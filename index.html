About me

Vitoria, Espirito Santo, Brazil

Vitória is the capital city of the state of Espírito Santo, located on a small island within a bay where several rivers meet the sea.

Founded in 1551, it is one of the oldest cities in Brazil and is connected to the mainland by impressive bridges.

The city is known for its high quality of life, beautiful beaches like Camburi, and its busy ports, which are vital for the Brazilian economy.
